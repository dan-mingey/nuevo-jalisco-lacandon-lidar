## 2. Ancient Maya Dating (Architecture, Ceramics & Hieroglyphic Panels)

1. **Ceramics / Excavated Stratigraphy:** None recovered to date (`0` excavations; `Tovalín 2020: 232`). Deeper Preclassic or Early Classic substructures (as found beneath Late Classic masonry at *Plan de Ayutla* and *Sak Tz'i'–Lacanjá Tzeltal*) remain untested.
2. **Standing Architecture — Late Classic (`c. AD 600–850`, specifically `c. AD 630–800` / 8th Century AD):**
   - **Usumacinta / Yaxchilán–Bonampak–Northern Lacanjá style** (`Carrasco 1981: 341–342`; `Ortiz & Tovalín 1998: 362–366`; `Tovalín et al. 2004: 56–58`; `Tovalín 2020: 239–241, 246`):
     - **Stepped / inverted-*Ik'* doorways** (*vanos escalonados / "Ik" invertido*) on Temples 2 and 4 — dated at Yaxchilán (Strs. 25, 26, 39, 41) to **`AD 630–750`** and at Bonampak (Str. 4) to the **8th century AD** (`Ortiz & Tovalín 1998: 366`).
     - **Maya corbelled vaults** (*bóveda maya*), monolithic stone lintels (red exterior stucco band on Temple 2), **perforated roofcomb** (*crestería* on Temple 2), **black-painted interior stucco** (Temples 2 & 4, shared with *Plan de Ayutla* Str. 13 and *Nuevo Chetumal* Str. 1), **cylindrical stone column-altars** inside Temples 2 (`0.40×0.90 m`) & 4 (`0.45×1.20 m`) matching Bonampak Strs. 4–8, and a **narrow front corridor + 3 rear rooms/*adoratorio*** plan linking Nuevo Jalisco to an **8th-century northern Lacanjá architectural group** (*Plan de Ayutla*, *Nuevo Chetumal*, *Nuevo Jalisco*, *Rancho Ojo de Agua*; `Tovalín 2020: 246`).
3. **Hieroglyphic Long Count & Calendar Round Dates on the *Nuevo Jalisco Panels* (`AD 717–732`):**
   - Stored in the **Bonampak INAH *bodega***; sketched by Ian Graham (*"at Bonampak; said to be from site near Nuevo Jalisco or Palestina"*) and attributed by custodian Arturo Contreras / Víctor Ortiz to nearby *"El Cedro"* (*Ojos de Agua–El Cedro*, which has a Type VII ballcourt) or *Plan de Ayutla* (`Bíró 2005: 11–15`; `Martos López 2009: 74`; `Tovalín 2020: 237`).
   - Exact chronology reconstructed by **Peter Mathews & Péter Bíró (`2005: 13–14`)**:
     - **Panel 2:** `9.14.5.5.0 *8 *Ajaw *3 *Pop` (**16 Feb AD 717**, accession/ritual `ukab'jiiy` supervised by **`K'ab' Chan Te'`, `Sak Tz'i' Ajaw`**) → `+4.13.0` → `9.14.10.0.0 5 Ajaw 3 Mak` (**13 Oct AD 721**, stone-binding & incense-scattering) → `9.14.15.0.0 11 Ajaw 18 Sak` (**17 Sep AD 726**, `wi'l ho'tuun` naming **`Xukalnaah`** and **`K'inich ?-B'ahlam`**).
     - **Panel 1:** Initial Series `9.14.17.4.7 *12 *Manik' 15 Muwan` (**2 Dec AD 728**, birth of **`?-B'ahlam`** [*"Knot-eye Jaguar"*] of **`Xukalnaah`**) → `+3.8.19` → **`9.15.0.13.6 *10 Kimi 14 Sek` (14 May AD 732**, death `och b'ijiiy` of `4 Winikhaab' Ajaw` **`Lam? K'inich Chaahk`**; dedicated shortly after **14 May 732**).

## 3. Two-Layer Resolution of *"Is It 'Missing'?"*

1. **The Ceremonial Core (`#08`, Gran Plaza & NE Acropolis):**
   - **Known to:** A handful of Chiapas specialists (`Tovalín/Ortiz` via the 2001 GPS point; `Golden/Scherer/Schroder` on `l0s473`) and Lacandón elders in *Lacanjá Chansayab* (`6.3 km` NE).
   - **Yet "missing" in four concrete senses:** (a) **absent from every public/academic digital atlas** (`EAAMS`, `INAH SIC`, `MARI-GISLAB`, `OSM`, `Wikidata` — while Carrasco's 1981 coordinates were `3.98 km` off and Tovalín's 2001 NAD27 UTM sat in a 1,000-copy print journal); (b) **unvisited by archaeologists for 25 years (`2001–2026`), closed to tourism, trail-less (`2.5 hr` bushwhack across 15 streams), and never excavated**; (c) **unknown physical survival today** (given severe structural cracks and `35°` leaning walls already in 2001); and (d) **never cleanly resolved in bare-earth LiDAR** (`Golden et al. 2016: 306` single-pass `LIDAR ANALYST` hillshade struggled with dense undergrowth; 2019 NCALM stopped `21 km` north) until **Britton's Nov 4, 2026 `0.33 m` reprocessing**.
2. **The Immediate Outlying Three-Mound Terrace Group (`LC-01`, `520 m` ENE at `16.72200° N, 91.16463° W`):**
   - **Unmapped on the ground and unpublished in archaeological maps:** On foot, Carrasco (`1981`) mapped only the ceremonial plaza, while Tovalín (`2004: 55`) noted in passing seeing dispersed low platforms along the approach route from Lacanjá-Chansayab (*"lo cual deberá corroborarse en un futuro próximo"*), and `Golden et al. (2016: 306)` explicitly reported *"we cannot identify smaller structures on the landscape surrounding Nuevo Jalisco in the lidar data due to lack of sufficient ground points."* In the `0.33 m` reprocessing (`38,258` raw `.laz` ground points across `320 × 320 m`), `LC-01` resolves cleanly as three aligned rectangular/rounded mounds (`+2.8 m` to `+3.5 m`, passing `360°` DEM closure) atop a `1,282 m²` (`< 2°`) leveled terrace.
